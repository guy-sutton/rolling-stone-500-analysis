# Rolling Stone's 500 Greatest Albums of All Time: A Data Analysis
This project explores trends in Rolling Stone’s “500 Greatest Albums of All Time.”  
Using **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib) in Jupyter Notebooks**, analysing release years, genres, artist appearances and the albums record labels.  

## 📊 Analysis
The notebook includes visualisations and insights into music history, allowing us to see:
- Most frequent genres across the list
- Distribution of albums across the decades
- Top artists with multiple albums in the Top 500
- Record labels dominating across what decade

## 🚀 Key Findings
- The 1970s produced the most Top 500 albums
- The Beatles were the most featured artists in the list
- Rock represnted just less than a quarter of all albums
- Few record label names appeared with names reappearing throughout
